Users are generally divided into two camps:
1. Super Users (su) or Administrators. Engineers can say "APCS, self killing please" and APCS will obey them
2. Users

Administrators possess full privileges, ranging from activating Emergency Mode to completely disabling the APCS.
Standard user privileges, by contrast, are restricted by the [configuration](/src/core/config.lua).

Users can be assigned via the [configuration](/src/core/config.lua) ([documentation](/src/en_US/config.md))
